Privacy

Only the information needed to run the opportunity index.

Public job listings are collected from their original sources and link back to those sources. We do not sell listing data or user account data.

Account information

If you create an account, Supabase stores your email address, matching profile, saved roles, feedback, and application-tracking choices. These records are associated with your account and are not public.

Retention and control

You can remove saved roles, feedback, and application records from your account. To request account deletion or report a privacy concern, open an issue in the project repository.
